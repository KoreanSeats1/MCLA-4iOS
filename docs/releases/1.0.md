# MCLA 4iOS 1.0

Custom touch controls, continuous driving slides, raised map/speed panels and the audited red-light glow correction for iPhone and iPad.

[README](https://github.com/KoreanSeats1/MCLA-4iOS#readme) · [Detailed changelog](https://github.com/KoreanSeats1/MCLA-4iOS/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) · [UI/feature guide](https://github.com/KoreanSeats1/MCLA-4iOS/blob/main/docs/FEATURES.md) · [Known issues](https://github.com/KoreanSeats1/MCLA-4iOS/blob/main/docs/KNOWN_BUGS.md)

## New in this release

- Scalable cyan native touch artwork, split gas/drift pedal and steering base/knob.
- Continuous Gas ↔ Gas + Handbrake ↔ Brake slides; the drift/brake midpoint combines all three driving inputs.
- Weight can retain the previous driving input during a slide and releases when the finger leaves its button.
- Approved M5 layout as the default, saved edits retained, editable positions/sizes and palette actions.
- Larger minimap at the upper left and speed/RPM at the upper right; padded map border uses the same transform as the fill.
- Fresh Weight, Lights, music-track and pause/play icons.
- Corrected two post-tonemap glow passes that changed red signals to blue/purple; Metal tests pass and M5 gameplay was user-confirmed.
- Expanded README, detailed changelog, feature guide and validation record.

## Included foundation

Native ARM64 AOT execution, a handwritten Metal backend and 603 offline native shader libraries; controller/touch/tilt support; save import/export; scene-resolution/filter/bloom/blur controls; optional spatial FSR 1; Retail Mode and bounded diagnostics; standard 30 FPS and experimental real-frame 60 FPS.

## Download and install

Download **MCLA-4iOS-1.0.ipa** and re-sign it with your own account using Sideloadly or AltStore Classic. **No original game files are included.** Supply your own complete Xbox 360 Complete Edition extraction in `MCLA_Game_Files` inside the MCLA Files container. The executable must sit directly in that folder. See [installation instructions](https://github.com/KoreanSeats1/MCLA-4iOS/blob/main/docs/INSTALLATION.md).

The IPA contains no personal provisioning profile and requires recipient signing. `SHA256SUMS.txt` and `release-manifest.json` identify the artifact and source commit. iOS/iPadOS 18 is the deployment minimum; gameplay testing is limited to Air/M5 and does not certify every compatible OS/device. Export your saves and preserve your signing account/bundle identifier when updating.

## Validation and limits

Version 1.0 is a milestone, not a claim of complete original-game accuracy. Controls were exercised on M5 and in the native harness. The padded-border transform regression passes and that build was installed on Air/M5; the final installed alignment screenshot was pending. The [validation record](https://github.com/KoreanSeats1/MCLA-4iOS/blob/main/docs/releases/1.0-validation.md) lists build, test and package evidence.

Two shader programs remain missing. Road shimmer, some checkpoint smoke/colors, fades, new-game ride height and broader warm-device/60 FPS behavior need work. Small blue/purple street-name signs exist in original source textures; green freeway boards are separate. Road sun-reflection math is present, but matching visual coverage at the same time/weather/view remains outstanding. No MetalFX frame generation, online-play acceptance or standard-build SMAA feature is claimed.
