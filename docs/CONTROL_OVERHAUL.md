# Experimental driving controls

Version 1.0 includes the work developed on `control-overhaul-test`, based on `control-overhaul`. The in-game HUD experiment and touch driving controls are enabled by default. Dedicated test preference keys leave the original branch’s enable/disable preferences untouched. Both switches remain available in **Driving Controls** for A/B testing. The original `control-overhaul` branch defaults the experiment off. `MCLA_CONTROL_OVERHAUL=1` temporarily selects it for a development launch; `0` forces the original layout.

The experiment adds left-side nitro, pause/camera buttons at the top, and gas, brake and combined gas/handbrake controls at the bottom right. The user’s October 5 button-sheet JPG is reconstructed as native vector artwork in `MCLAControlArtwork.h`: translucent cyan double rings, core icons, dotted pedals, normal/pressed/disabled states, and a separate joystick base and knob. The artwork applies to both layouts and the rearrangeable palette. Existing action bindings and steering input sensitivity are retained; no new shift/look-back bindings are invented from the artwork. Tilt steering still replaces the joystick when enabled. Other actions remain available through the existing pad/palette. Experimental positions, sizes and active controls use separate `…Overhaul` preference keys. Reset only affects the selected layout. Switching layouts keeps the new artwork, retains the original button bindings/titles internally and releases held input. The experimental gas/handbrake lower zone remains a separate editable control. Its default position joins the gas zone across device aspect ratios; moving either zone saves its independent position. When aligned, they draw a shared split-pedal outline; separated zones draw complete outlines.

## HUD isolation

The renderer evaluates the existing audited 2D HUD vertex transform for vertex shader `F8B6972A1D56B354`, including its different fragment and stencil layers. It reads draw bounds in the movie's 1280×720 coordinate system before safe framing. Complete draws inside the lower-left mini map or lower-right gauge regions qualify. Mixed triangle/quad batches of up to 4,096 guest vertices are inspected per independent primitive so map rims can move with the map even when batched with other HUD elements; their index slices and order are preserved. The map is scaled to 90% and anchored at x=28, y=76; the gauges retain their original size, anchored at y=76 with a 28-pixel right margin. This makes the panels about 15% and 18% larger than the initial prototype. These transforms apply to viewport and scissor together. Qualified panels use the device’s upper-left and upper-right corners, retaining the authored pixel density instead of the centered safe frame’s top gutter on iPad. Other HUD draws retain their existing safe frame. Original guest memory, textures and UI resources are untouched.

Inline and indexed vertex reads are range checked and bounded to 4,096 vertices. Full-screen backgrounds, invalid geometry, unrelated shaders and other logical target sizes remain in place. Mixed batches using other topologies or more than 4,096 vertices retain the conservative whole-draw policy. With diagnostics enabled, `MCLA_HUD_TRACE_FRAME=<frame>` also emits `MCLA HUD MOVE` bounds, group and transform information for that frame.

Map border correction: the live M5 trace (token 610061, frame 9210, draw 1728) identifies a padded ring quad at `(23.3,407.6)..(359.8,744.2)`, outside the fill's conservative bounds. The Oct 6 correction recognizes the padded square's rotating bounding box around the original map center, plus small GPS decorations around its footprint. These layers receive the exact existing minimap translation and 90% scale. Regressions use the captured bounds and test all 360 headings for both the border and an orbiting GPS pointer; fill/border/arrow transforms remain identical. Opt-in render-probe captures also trace HUD geometry for their capture frame.

This is a spatial renderer experiment, not semantic identification of a game movie. The same shader also draws menus. In gameplay, masks or text using other programs and batches spanning both panels may remain behind; corner menu elements may qualify. Verify the map circle, rotating streets, route arrow, gauge digits/needle, nitro, race overlays and pause/GPS/garage screens before merging. The user confirmed the controls and corrected light glows in M5 gameplay. The final padded-border build was installed on Air/M5; its post-install alignment screenshot was pending. Broad menu/race HUD coverage and performance measurements remain unverified.

## October 5 saved layout and slide controls

The M5 preference file was retrieved before editing; its experimental positions and 12 deployed actions are now the factory defaults. Saved edits still win. Gas and handbrake had no saved overrides, so their automatic docking is retained.

`MCLASlidingControls.h` owns each finger across Gas, Gas + Handbrake, Brake and Weight. Cyan connectors follow the actual edited frames. The central brake/drift bridge combines RT, A and LT; entering Weight preserves that finger’s last driving combination and adds B only while inside the visible Weight button. Sliding off releases it. Multiple fingers merge independently, and cancellation, layout editing, inactive app state and mode changes release routed inputs. Other action controls keep their existing touch handling.

Weight now uses a car with lateral arrows, lights a headlamp with beams, track controls mirrored skip icons, and pause a combined pause/play icon. The native preview passed continuous transitions, the three-input blend, Weight hold/release, independent fingers, cancellation and saved-layout reset isolation. Its latest layout and artwork are under `artifacts/control-overhaul/sliding/`. The preview uses captured HUD crops and does not prove the live outline fix.

## Tiled color mapping test

This branch now composes the reference renderer’s host-channel mapping for decoded tiled RGBA8 surfaces with endian mode 2 and guest swizzle `0x60A`. Those grading/noise surfaces retain decoded RGB instead of receiving another R/B exchange. Linear light-color/index tables retain their guest R/B exchange. Other formats, endian modes and swizzles retain their prior mapping, and produced render targets retain their existing sampling path. `MCLA_TILED_COLOR_SWIZZLE=0` restores the earlier mapping for a development comparison.

The captured light-glow pair `ACBA71021301E1DF / 35F41762995C91B9` receives red `FFFF0000`; the vertex shader’s `.zyxw` correctly yields red with the current packed-color format. A blanket vertex or fragment recolor would therefore conceal rather than establish the cause. The channel-composition change follows the reference renderer’s measured tiled/linear distinction. A real Metal texture-view test preserves red, gold, green, blue and alpha in both storage paths. The user confirmed blue/purple lights persist with this mapping; it did not resolve the reported glow defect. It does not establish a fix for missing pavement reflections.

## Red signal glow correction

Live M5 pixel history isolated two additive passes after tone mapping. At the active red signal, tone mapping produces `(207,78,78)`, then glow PS `92CAA8C0D85D8EC2` adds blue to `(207,78,129)` and glow PS `B2BE9BE7339A92A7` produces `(207,78,245)`. Their vertex shaders `F52B50DA9C0F8997` and `1B7B507E54AADA8A` pass COLOR0 through as `xyzw`, unlike the bloom seed’s explicit `zyxw` fetch.

The packed normalized declaration retains its BGRA view for those two observed glow shaders. Other shader inputs keep the existing correction. Actual Metal tests exercise both glow paths and normalized/integer seed paths with red, yellow, green and blue. The diagnostic source-color budget increases from 64 to 128 unique declarations to include the late glow passes.

The captured Wilcox/Selma signs use a blue/white BC1 atlas; decoding the actual guest texture shows the same blue background before material rendering. That scene does not establish a green-to-purple sign conversion. The road sun-specular calculation and nonzero height/specular texture remain present; equivalent sunset lighting/view conditions still need comparison.

Installed M5 validation: the corrected gameplay frame shows a red signal glow and warm headlights; the user confirms the lights seem fixed. This validates the observed glow correction, with broader effect coverage still pending. The user continues to report street-sign color problems, which remain separate from this fix.

Reference comparison: fresh M5 capture token 610058 shows a blue/purple LA BREA AVE nameplate beside red signals. The extracted guest BC1 atlas contains that nameplate with a blue/purple background. Original console gameplay at https://images.cgames.de/images/gsgp/226/gp12-t-midnight-club-los-angeles05_1217973.jpg also shows blue street-name signs (Hollywood Blvd). The user requested matching the original game, so no green recolor is applied. This does not validate every sign material: green freeway/directional boards are a separate asset class. The follow-up token 610059 pixel history sampled a different scene after the car moved and is not sign-color evidence.
