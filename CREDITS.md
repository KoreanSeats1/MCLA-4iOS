# Credits and acknowledgements

MCLA 4iOS stands on years of work by the original game developers, reverse engineers, emulator authors, recompilation developers and library maintainers. Thank you to everyone who made this possible.

Rockstar San Diego and Rockstar Games created Midnight Club: Los Angeles. All original game assets and trademarks belong to their respective owners. They are not included in this project.

## Project lineage

See the [README attribution table](README.md#credits-attribution-and-license) and [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for roles, licenses and dependency scope. KoreanSeats1 maintains this iOS integration. The catalog below includes inherited fork history; an entry does not imply that person directly worked on this iOS port.

ReXGlue also explicitly acknowledges [Tom (crack)](https://github.com/tomcl7), [Loreaxe](https://github.com/Loreaxe), [Mystixor](https://github.com/Mystixor), [Graine25](https://github.com/Graine25), [Carlos Estrague](https://github.com/mrcmunir), [sanjay900](https://github.com/sanjay900), [Toby](https://github.com/TbyDtch) and [Roxxsen](https://github.com/Roxxsen). Consult its upstream README for individual roles.
